1. Gate 1 — Data Freshness & Earnings Normalization

The July 31, 2026 Value Line report projected 2026 revenue of $485B and EPS of $14.25, 2027 EPS of $15.00, and 2029–31 EPS of $21.50. It also showed a 26× projected average P/E, A++ Financial Strength, Safety 2 and Earnings Predictability 70.

Alphabet's subsequently filed June 2026 10-Q materially changes how the earnings number must be interpreted. Q2 revenue was $119.8B, up 24%; Google Services revenue increased 15%, while Google Cloud revenue increased 82%. However, other income included approximately $99B of net gains on equity securities, principally from SpaceX and another private-company investment. Those gains are not a sustainable operating earnings stream.

Accordingly, this report rejects FAST Graphs' 2026 $20.60 EPS estimate as a clean valuation denominator and anchors the forward model primarily to approximately $15 of normalized 2027 EPS.

11. Adversarial Analyst

The 19× illusion: the headline P/E is contaminated by enormous equity-security gains. A normalized earnings denominator produces a meaningfully higher multiple.

AI is not free: first-half capex reached roughly $80.6B, Q2 capex alone was $44.9B, and long-term data-center lease commitments are substantial. The key question is return on incremental AI capital, not merely revenue growth.

Dilution and financing: Alphabet issued common stock, debt and mandatory convertible preferred securities in 2026 to fund infrastructure and general corporate purposes. That weakens the historical narrative of a company funding everything internally while shrinking the share count.

Search concentration: AI appears expansionary today, but Search remains the central cash engine. Competitive disruption or materially worse AI-search economics would still affect the entire valuation.

Bear-case weakness: the evidence does not support declaring Search structurally broken. Services is growing, Cloud is accelerating, liquidity is immense and AI is generating real commercial demand. Therefore SELL is not supported.
