12. Adversarial Analyst

Customer concentration: custom AI silicon creates deep relationships but also bargaining power and insourcing/diversification risk. Value Line specifically flags Google's potential use of MediaTek for part of its TPU supply.

Peak-growth risk: Value Line expects 70% EPS growth in FY2026 and another 67% in FY2027. Those rates cannot be extrapolated indefinitely. The valuation must work after growth normalizes — which is why the central Back-Calc uses 20%, not 40%+.

Debt: VMware transformed the balance sheet. Total debt remains around $59B and Value Line shows debt at 61% of capital on its measure. Strong cash generation mitigates the risk, but leverage reduces flexibility compared with a net-cash semiconductor peer.

Semiconductor cyclicality: AI infrastructure spending is currently extraordinary. A hyperscaler capex pause, financing constraint or architectural shift could produce a sharp estimate reset and P/E compression simultaneously.

Bear-case weakness: a credible bear must explain why AI demand, custom accelerators, networking and VMware all weaken enough to push long-run growth toward low double digits. Current evidence does not support that as the base case.
