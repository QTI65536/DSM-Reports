15. Adversarial Analyst

The strongest bull case is unusually credible: Costco combines recurring membership economics, traffic growth, strong digital growth, international runway, exceptional financial strength and consistent execution. The adversarial objection is almost entirely price. At roughly 43× fiscal EPS, a buyer needs substantial earnings growth and continued premium valuation simultaneously. Multiple compression to still-generous 35×–40× levels absorbs enough of the operating growth that the normalized 2033 scenarios fall below the 10% hurdle. Special dividends could improve returns but are deliberately excluded because they cannot be scheduled reliably.
