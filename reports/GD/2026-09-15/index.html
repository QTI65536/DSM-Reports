14. Adversarial Analyst

The strongest objection is not business quality but starting valuation. GD's recent price CAGR has materially exceeded EPS CAGR, and both normalized 2033 cases remain just under the hurdle. Marine's backlog is attractive but shipbuilding execution, labor availability and cost control can constrain margin conversion; Combat and Technologies grew much more slowly than Aerospace and Marine in Q2. Goodwill is also significant at $20.9B. Against that, the bull case survives scrutiny: backlog, book-to-bill, balance-sheet quality and predictability are unusually strong. The framework therefore calls for price discipline rather than a structural rejection.
