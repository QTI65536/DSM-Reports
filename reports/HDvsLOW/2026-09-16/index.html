8. Bull Case Steel Man

HD: SRS and the Pro ecosystem broaden the contractor opportunity, digital fulfillment continues to improve, Q2 comps strengthened to 1.7%, and a housing/remodeling normalization could restore mid/high-single-digit EPS growth. Cash generation supports patience. These arguments survive scrutiny.

LOW: valuation has compressed, Pro/Home Services/online are outperforming, acquisitions broaden the professional channel, and first-half cash generation supports debt reduction. A housing recovery could produce attractive returns from the current multiple. These arguments also survive as operating and valuation observations, but cannot override the mandatory structural gate.

9. Adversarial Comparison

Both companies remain exposed to low housing turnover, affordability pressure and weak big-ticket DIY demand. HD carries the higher current earnings multiple, while LOW appears cheaper. But LOW's apparent valuation advantage is accompanied by much weaker FAST Graphs Financial Strength, negative book equity and a V4.2.5 Structural Rot failure. HD's principal risk is different: investors are paying roughly 20× earnings while the current FG growth estimate is only 1.66%, so the normalized investment case requires an actual earnings recovery rather than mere multiple stability.
