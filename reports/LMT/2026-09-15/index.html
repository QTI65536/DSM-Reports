14. Adversarial Analyst

The strongest counterargument is execution and balance-sheet sensitivity, not demand. The June 10-Q still identifies program-risk exposures: first-half unfavorable adjustments included F-16, C-130, Heavy Lift and Seahawk programs, while cumulative losses on one classified Aeronautics program remained about $1.8B. Fixed-price development structures can shift technical and schedule risk to LMT. Liabilities equal roughly 86% of assets and debt/capital remains high. In addition, recent price CAGR has exceeded EPS CAGR. These risks do not negate the $230B backlog or current cash-flow strength, but they make the unresolved SQ valuation check important before certifying a BUY.
