15. Adversarial Analyst

The strongest bull argument is that negative equity reflects historic capital-allocation choices rather than operational insolvency: cash generation is substantial, liquidity remains available, and management is deleveraging. That argument survives as an economic observation. It does not survive the controlling V4.2.5 Structural Qualification rule, which explicitly treats liabilities above 90% of assets as Structural Rot for an ordinary operating company. Meanwhile, organic demand is weak—Q2 comps were only 0.2%, FY comps are now guided flat, and adjusted EPS is roughly stagnant. Thus the framework's objection is both structural and cyclical, even though the market valuation has become more reasonable.
