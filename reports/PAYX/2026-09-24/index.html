15. Adversarial Analyst

The strongest objection is no longer obvious overvaluation; it is execution and balance-sheet change after Paycor. Fiscal 2026 revenue growth was heavily acquisition-assisted, debt rose to fund the ~$4.1B transaction, and interest expense increased materially. Management Solutions grew only 4% in Q1, while the higher-growth PEO/Insurance business is a smaller part of revenue. The dividend payout is also high. AI is a two-sided variable: WISE may strengthen retention and productivity, but AI can also lower interface and workflow barriers for competitors. Against those risks, Q1 margin expansion, 10% adjusted-EPS growth, 95 Predictability and major price/earnings compression are substantial positives. The remaining DSM obstacle is the unresolved formal SQ valuation input, not a weak base-case Back-Calc.
