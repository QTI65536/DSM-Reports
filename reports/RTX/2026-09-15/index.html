14. Adversarial Analyst

RTX combines high-quality franchises with unusually strong demand, but the market has already capitalized much of that improvement. The FAST Graphs Vertical Wall—price CAGR near 19% versus EPS CAGR near 11%—is the clearest warning. Pratt & Whitney still faces supply-chain and original-equipment margin pressures, and the balance sheet contains roughly $84B of goodwill/intangibles. A $289B backlog improves visibility but does not guarantee margin realization. Against that, the bull case is unusually concrete: backlog is growing, all three segments are expanding, FCF guidance is strong, and the commercial/defense mix reduces dependence on a single end market. The DSM objection is therefore valuation rather than business quality.
