12. Adversarial Analyst

Growth duration remains unproven: one strong year does not erase a long period of near-flat earnings. The FAST Graphs historical growth reading is only 0.18%.

Debt remains substantial: June 2026 long-term debt was about $143.4B and total debt roughly $165.2B. Even with 2.5× net unsecured debt/adjusted EBITDA, leverage limits strategic flexibility.

Buybacks require scrutiny: Verizon raised its 2026 repurchase target to as much as $4.5B. At current valuation this is not obviously destructive, but debt reduction competes for the same capital.

Multiple expansion is doing work in the bull case: the only scenario clearly above the 10% hurdle uses a 12× terminal P/E, well above the recent 8.45× normal multiple.

Counterweight: the bearish case should not ignore the real 2026 cash-flow acceleration, improved subscriber momentum and strong dividend coverage. Those factors argue against SELL and support HOLD for an established position.
